# H06 — enrollment sender gate

**Replaces/amends:** ARCHITECTURE.md v0.3 §§2.2, 3.2, 5.3, 7 (P3k), 9.

## Drop-in replacement text

### §2.2 — enrollment receiver

Keep the exported EnrollmentSetupReceiver declaration with android:permission="android.permission.DUMP" and no intent filter. Android checks the permission on the sender. This is a privileged-sender gate, not proof of a shell or USB sender: it trusts shell and every other UID holding DUMP, including platform and privileged holders and a third-party app that declares DUMP and receives a development grant through privileged tooling such as adb shell pm grant. An ordinary app cannot grant itself DUMP. The receiver accepts no credentials.

### §3.2 — supervised trigger

During attended USB setup, with the kiosk visible and debugging temporarily authorized, kiosk-manager sends no extras or secrets:

    adb shell am broadcast -n com.example.webkiosk/.remote.EnrollmentSetupReceiver -a com.example.webkiosk.action.BEGIN_ENROLLMENT

The receiver requires the exact action, no extras and this app as active Device Owner. On API 34+, if getSentFromUid() is available, require SHELL_UID; reject any other known UID. INVALID_UID is accepted only under the system-enforced DUMP gate above. Android 15 AOSP am broadcast offers no identity-sharing option and leaves sharing off, so its sender UID is unavailable. Do not treat INVALID_UID as evidence of shell or USB. A DUMP-capable sender can open the panel without USB; control and audit all DUMP grants.

Signal only the resumed, focused MainActivity in-process; otherwise fail and require a retry. Require a person at the tablet to tap the panel's local **Start** before key generation or enrollment begins. Retain Cancel, the five-minute expiry, focus-loss and process-death closure, support/input blocking, physical fingerprint and request-ID comparison, and authenticated admin approval. Start is a human-presence factor, not staff authentication; it does not replace the comparison or admin approval. No student-facing trigger, WebView bridge, remote command or intent extra starts enrollment.

### §5.3 and §7 P3k — setup and K11 gate

At setup, inventory and audit DUMP holders for every relevant user, including system and privileged packages and development grants; record package, UID, grant and disposition against an approved baseline. Enumerate installed packages and inspect their granted permissions with dumpsys package, including per-user grants; use pm list permissions -f to inspect the permission definition, not as a holder inventory. Investigate unexpected holders and remove unauthorized grants or packages before enrollment. Repeat the audit after changes to packages or grants and before subsequent enrollment. Record device build and evidence. After setup disconnect USB, disable USB and wireless debugging, and revoke debugging authorizations; verify lockdown. Re-enable only for supervised maintenance.

P3k must prove the shell route, ordinary-app denial, holder inventory and debugging lockdown on the deployed K11 image. Failure blocks this enrollment route and release; do not bypass the gate.

### §9 — reconciliation

Replace the v0.3 enrollment-receiver rationale: DUMP gates senders with that permission, not shell alone. Android 15's shell broadcast does not share identity; the local Start tap and physical comparison remain mandatory. The inventory and debugging lockdown bound the privileged-sender trust.

AOSP Android 15 sources: [broadcast delivery](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/android-15.0.0_r1/services/core/java/com/android/server/am/BroadcastQueueImpl.java), [DUMP definition](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/android-15.0.0_r1/core/res/AndroidManifest.xml), [shell manifest](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/android-15.0.0_r1/packages/Shell/AndroidManifest.xml), [shell command options](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/android-15.0.0_r1/services/core/java/com/android/server/am/ActivityManagerShellCommand.java), and [identity-sharing default](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/android-15.0.0_r1/core/java/android/app/BroadcastOptions.java).

## Acceptance tests:

- On K11, run the exact broadcast during supervised setup; record delivery and sender UID, verify the panel opens only while resumed and focused, and verify no key generation before local Start. Complete comparison and admin approval. Reject malformed action/extras and known non-shell UID.
- From a separate ordinary app without DUMP, send the explicit broadcast; verify system denial and no panel. In a controlled test, grant a DUMP-declaring test app using privileged tooling and demonstrate the gate's trust boundary, then revoke the grant and verify denial. Do not infer sender identity from INVALID_UID.
- Enumerate installed packages and actual DUMP grants for all relevant users, compare with the approved baseline, resolve unexpected holders and retain the audit. Verify shell's grant and the device's broadcast behavior; an OEM difference fails the gate until reconciled.
- Disconnect USB, disable USB and wireless debugging, revoke authorizations and verify settings and that an old host cannot issue ADB commands. Check Cancel, expiry, focus loss and process death prevent enrollment without a fresh trigger and Start.
