## Drop-in replacement Markdown

### Authorization and support policy

Roles are cumulative: admin ⊇ operator ⊇ viewer. Check current session, role and device scope at admission and immediately before dispatch; fail closed on unavailable authoritative state or audit. An attachment is the exclusive device lease. `kiosk` approval is requested on attach; it does not become a grant until the tablet approves. A valid grant is unexpired, unrevoked and bound to the current session, device and kiosk attachment; pending, denied, expired, stopped or disconnected is **no grant**. A status attachment has no local grant. An invalid or absent lease never authorizes a command. The tablet independently rechecks its local grant and boundary before execution.

The following is the complete authorization table. `*` means every value in that column; rows are disjoint. Role means minimum role. `G` means a valid local grant; `N` means no valid grant. `—` means no attachment required; attachment and grant do not confer extra authority. All other combinations deny. No policy bypasses scope, live authorization, audit, deadlines, device capability or the kiosk boundary.

| Op | Role | Attachment mode | Grant | `REMOTE_SUPPORT_POLICY` | Decision / additional conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `list_devices` | viewer | * | * | * | Allow; only in-scope devices and content-free online/offline/maintenance state. |
| `attach{status}` | viewer | — | N | * | Allow request in scope; exclusive lease rules apply. |
| `attach{kiosk}` | viewer | — | N | * | Allow request in scope; local approval is required before content or commands. |
| `detach` | viewer | status or kiosk | * | * | Allow only for owning session and attachment. |
| frames | viewer | kiosk | G | * | Allow after banner and boundary checks. |
| `tap`, `swipe`, `long_press`, `kiosk_back`, `kiosk_home`, `reload_kiosk` | operator | kiosk | G | * | Allow; input and navigation remain kiosk-scoped. Coordinate input also requires valid frame binding. |
| `lock_now`, `reboot`, `enter_kiosk` | admin | kiosk | G | * | Allow. |
| `kiosk_home`, `reload_kiosk` | operator | status | N | `status_admin` | Allow. |
| `lock_now`, `reboot` | admin | status | N | `status_admin` | Allow. |
| `enter_kiosk` | admin | status | N | * | Allow only to end an active maintenance window; otherwise deny. This is a recovery action, not a general unattended command. |

The four `status_admin` exceptions are exclusively `reload_kiosk`, `kiosk_home`, `lock_now` and `reboot` on a status lease. `enter_kiosk` without a grant is exclusively the active-maintenance recovery row, under either policy. In particular, neither policy allows unattended wipe or exit. A kiosk attachment with pending or lost consent is not a status lease and receives no exception. Approval of a support session is local, session-specific consent; step-up and desktop confirmation do not replace it. Invalid role/scope yields `FORBIDDEN`, missing lease `LEASE_REQUIRED`, missing grant `GRANT_REQUIRED`, and policy or feature-gate denial `DISABLED_BY_POLICY`; malformed or unsupported operations follow the §4 registry. Denials never dispatch.

### Navigation

`kiosk_home` takes no arguments and loads only the compiled `equity_home` destination (`START_URL`) after the canonical §2.2 URI policy check. `kiosk_back` takes no arguments. On the WebView's owning thread, check `canGoBack()`, inspect the immediately previous item from `copyBackForwardList()`, and validate its URL under the same URI policy immediately before `goBack()`. If no prior item, invalid or missing URL, changed history, or uncertain validation, do not navigate; return a negative acknowledgement. Never skip to another history item, invoke the existing Back fallback, launch HOME or another activity, or accept a remote URL. Continue to enforce the §5.7 POST/redirect release gate; a history snapshot alone does not establish confinement. Android documents the history snapshot and owning-thread requirements in [WebView](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/webkit/WebView).

## Acceptance tests:

- Exhaustively generate op × role × mode (none/status/kiosk) × grant (valid/absent/expired) × policy cases against the table, including cumulative roles, missing scope, expired lease and all four status exceptions; assert denied commands never reach the tablet. Test pending kiosk approval under `status_admin` and every exception on the wrong lease.
- Under both policies, test `enter_kiosk` with a status lease and no grant in active and inactive maintenance; only active recovery succeeds. Loss of lease or scope before dispatch denies it.
- For each destructive op, independently remove local consent, feature enablement, confirmation, fresh step-up and admin scope; assert denial and no dispatch. Exercise grant revocation and step-up reuse between admission and dispatch; verify intent audit precedes dispatch and failed audit blocks it. Verify exit's separate recovery gate.
- On the actual WebView, exercise empty, allowed, disallowed and changed history, including redirect and POST confinement release checks. Back never launches an activity; home accepts no fields and loads only `equity_home`. Reject URL-like and extra arguments and reserved `navigate`.
