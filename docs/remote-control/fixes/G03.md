# G03: frame binding and gesture containment

**Replaces/amends:** ARCHITECTURE.md v0.2 §§2.2 (Kiosk boundary), 2.3 (viewer), 4 (wire protocol, frames, admission and errors), 5.1 (screen view), 7 (P3k checkpoint).

## Drop-in replacement

- **Capture and geometry.** For each approved attachment, `KioskA11yService` resolves the verified foreground `MainActivity` window and WebView bounds in screen coordinates. It records the visible native Chrome and WiFi controls, banner, Stop and any other overlapping native control as excluded rectangles, including their complete hit areas. Unknown or unmeasurable bounds fail closed. Select a nonempty axis-aligned rectangle wholly inside the visible WebView and disjoint from every exclusion; this is the content-area rect for this generation. Crop the window screenshot to precisely that rectangle before JPEG encoding; no excluded pixels enter the JPEG. If screenshot-to-screen mapping, clipping, scaling or exclusion cannot be established, send no frame and disable touch. Do not infer safety from the screenshot: window capture may omit accessibility overlays.
- A geometry generation changes on window, focus, bounds, exclusion, scaling, rotation or visibility change. Invalidation revokes the grant as in §2.2, clears frames and input, and requires fresh local approval. Capture rechecks the boundary after the screenshot callback before retaining or sending it. Close each `ScreenshotResult` hardware buffer after use.
- Retain a per-attachment ring of the last **8** successfully captured frame records: `{attachment_id, grant_id, frame_id, generation, window_id, capture_uptime_ms, content_rect_screen, excluded_rects_screen}`. IDs are never reused within an attachment. `capture_uptime_ms` is `ScreenshotResult.getTimestamp()`; compare only with tablet `SystemClock.uptimeMillis()`, never a desktop or relay clock. Clear the ring on invalidation, detach, disconnect or grant expiry. Suspend/screen lock invalidates the grant; uptime excludes sleep. A frame header carries `{attachment_id, grant_id, frame_id, generation}` and JPEG dimensions; the timestamp is tablet-local metadata, not authority supplied by the desktop.
- **Display boundary.** A frame is displayed only after the Tk UI thread decodes it, installs it in the visible viewer widget and commits that widget update. Receipt, relay forwarding and decode alone are not display. On each replacement the desktop immediately disables input, installs the frame, then sends `frame_displayed{attachment_id, grant_id, frame_id, generation}` on its authenticated WSS before enabling input for that frame. Clear the displayed reference on hide, detach, disconnect or decode/render failure. Map pointer positions through the actual displayed image bounds, rejecting letterbox clicks; never clamp. The relay verifies the attachment and forwards this acknowledgement in order to the tablet. The tablet accepts only a retained matching frame; acknowledgement of an older or unknown frame cannot roll back the latest accepted display reference. The relay serializes acknowledgements and commands for the attachment on the device socket. Frame replacement or ack/command races may reject input; they never authorize an older frame.
- **Coordinate commands on both hops.** Staff → relay: `cmd{id, device_id, attachment_id, grant_id, frame_id, generation, op, args}`. Relay → tablet: `cmd{id, attachment_id, grant_id, frame_id, generation, op, args, deadline_ms}`. For `tap` and `long_press`, `args={x,y}`; for `swipe`, `args={x1,y1,x2,y2,ms}`. All coordinates are finite numbers in [0,1], relative to the JPEG crop; `ms` is bounded by the protocol's gesture limits. The relay preserves the binding unchanged and checks the current attachment, session, policy, lease and grant. Other operations retain their applicable fields and authorization rules; these frame fields are mandatory for coordinate commands, not a substitute for authorization.
- **Tablet validation.** Immediately before dispatch, atomically check the live attachment and local grant, deadline, foreground window, visible banner and current boundary. Find the ring record matching all four IDs; require it to be the latest accepted desktop display acknowledgement, same current generation, window, content rect and exclusions. Require `0 <= uptimeMillis() - capture_uptime_ms <= 1000`; missing, future or expired timestamps fail closed. Convert normalized coordinates to screen pixels using the retained crop rect, with 1 mapping inside its last pixel. Reject a path unless every point of the actual gesture, including its endpoints and any interpolation, lies inside the current and retained content rect and outside all exclusions. Recheck immediately before dispatch; serialize validation, invalidation and dispatch so queued work cannot cross a generation change. Do not trust relay time, coordinates, or a claimed display without the acknowledgement and local record.
- **Stale errors.** Return `ATTACHMENT_STALE` for a replaced or released attachment, `GRANT_STALE` for a missing or replaced grant, `FRAME_UNKNOWN` for a missing/evicted frame or unaccepted display acknowledgement, `FRAME_NOT_DISPLAYED` for a reference superseded by a displayed frame, `FRAME_EXPIRED` for age outside the interval, `GENERATION_STALE` for changed geometry/window, and `GESTURE_OUT_OF_BOUNDS` for an invalid path. Reject without dispatch; do not retry automatically. Malformed fields use `INVALID_MESSAGE`.
- **Queues and deduplication.** Scope command queues and dedup keys to `(attachment_id, command_id)` at relay and tablet. Reserve atomically, retain pending and terminal outcomes for the attachment lifetime, reject a reused ID with different content as `DUPLICATE_ID_CONFLICT`, and reject new IDs if retention is full. Maintain the existing per-device queue cap of 32 and per-session rate limit. On attachment invalidation, detach, replacement, disconnect or lease loss, discard its queued commands and frame acknowledgements; no work migrates to a new attachment. A dispatched command without an outcome is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` and is never replayed. Coordinate-command expiry is checked again at dispatch.
- **In-flight gestures / K11 gate.** On focus or geometry invalidation, immediately block new dispatch and revoke the grant; cancel an in-flight gesture only if a demonstrably safe cancellation mechanism exists and is verified on the deployed K11. Android `dispatchGesture` cancels existing gestures when dispatching another gesture, but that is not a documented standalone cancel API and a replacement gesture must not be assumed safe. No cancellation gesture may reach excluded or newly focused UI. If containment from invalidation through completion cannot be proven on the deployed build, disable remote touch for that build; capture may remain available if independently safe. `dispatchGesture` reports submission, not successful completion: use its callback for completion/cancellation, and treat an absent outcome as unknown.

Android references: [AccessibilityService](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/accessibilityservice/AccessibilityService), [GestureDescription](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/accessibilityservice/GestureDescription), [ScreenshotResult](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/accessibilityservice/AccessibilityService.ScreenshotResult). Window capture requires API 34 and screenshot capability; gesture dispatch requires gesture capability and is affected by accessibility features such as magnification.

## Acceptance tests:

- On the K11, inspect decoded JPEG pixels and exercise taps and swipes at every crop edge and across Chrome, WiFi, banner and Stop; no excluded control is visible or activated. Unknown geometry sends no frame and accepts no touch.
- Exercise displayed-frame acknowledgements, delayed/reordered frames, frame replacement, ring eviction, mismatched IDs, expired/future timestamps and a one-second boundary on the tablet clock; each invalid reference receives its specified rejection without dispatch.
- Detach and reattach during queued and in-flight work; old acknowledgements, commands and duplicate IDs cannot affect the new attachment. Verify duplicate and conflict outcomes within one attachment and queue overflow.
- During long press and swipe, change focus, rotate/change geometry, obscure the window and press Stop. Verify no gesture reaches the new or excluded UI through completion; otherwise build with touch disabled. Verify capture and grant invalidation independently.
