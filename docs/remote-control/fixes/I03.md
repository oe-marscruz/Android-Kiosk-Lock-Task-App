# I03 — Terminal messages and execution deadlines

**Replaces/amends:** ARCHITECTURE.md v0.4 §§2.1 (limits), 2.2 (tablet lease and invalidation), 3.3 (grant, lifecycle and step-up dispatch), 4 (envelope, message lists, deadline and admission).

## Drop-in Markdown

### Grant and terminal events (§§2.1, 2.2, 3.3)

- `GRANT_MAX_S=300`. On local Allow, the tablet starts a fixed grant deadline at `elapsedRealtime() + GRANT_MAX_S`; it reports the remaining duration in `support_granted`. The relay starts its own deadline at receipt, capped by that duration. Both independently require a live lease and grant. Neither lease renewal, same-mode attach nor substantive operations renews the grant. After expiry, staff must request a new kiosk attachment and obtain fresh local approval. Wall-clock changes have no effect.
- On tablet lease expiry in either mode, release the attachment and send unsolicited `attachment_expired{attachment_id, reason:"lease_expired"}`. On kiosk grant expiry or local Stop, release it and send unsolicited `support_stopped{attachment_id, reason:"grant_expired"|"local_stop"}`. Local invalidation and cleanup happen first, even if the connection is unavailable; no delivery or retry is required after disconnect.
- On an authenticated current device socket, the relay serializes terminal events under the device lock. A matching live attachment is released, its grant, queued work and frame state invalidated, and its owner notified with `attachment_ended` or `support_stopped`. Duplicate or stale IDs are harmless and cannot affect a replacement; disconnect independently releases the attachment. A dispatched command without a terminal result is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` and is never replayed.

### IDs and results (§4)

- Envelope `id` is solely request/reply correlation; replies echo it and unsolicited events use `null`. A staff `cmd` contains a separate `command_id`; relay-to-device `cmd` carries that same `command_id`. Deduplication is by `(attachment_id, command_id)`, never envelope `id`.
- Device terminal result is unsolicited `ack{command_id, attachment_id, ok, err}` (`err` null on success). A tablet may send it after release for work already dispatched. The relay accepts it only from the authenticated device connection associated with the recorded dispatch, matches both IDs, and records the terminal result once without restoring authority or forwarding scoped content to a replacement attachment. If already reported `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, record the late result in audit; do not silently revise the reported result. Retain dispatched correlation and dedup tombstones beyond release for a bounded result window; after that, discard late acks. Never replay an unknown outcome.

### Wipe reservation (§§3.3, 4)

`exit_kiosk` is reserved and denied in v1. `wipe` is the only step-up operation. Ordinary commands retain §4's receipt-relative `deadline_ms` and transit assumption; they do not use reservations.

1. At admission the relay starts its monotonic command deadline. After ordinary authorization and before consuming step-up, it sends `reserve{command_id, op:"wipe", attachment_id, ttl_ms}` with a positive TTL no greater than the remaining command deadline and `CMD_DEADLINE_S`. The tablet verifies the current kiosk attachment, lease, grant and local policy, atomically deduplicates the command, creates an unpredictable single-use `reservation_id` bound to the command, attachment and device connection, and sets its own monotonic expiry to receipt plus `ttl_ms`. It replies `reserved{command_id, attachment_id, reservation_id}` with the reserve envelope ID.
2. At the execute writer, under I02's ordering, the relay rechecks the live binding, authorization, gate, its command deadline and the matching reservation; then consumes the step-up, durably audits intent and performs a bounded send of `execute{reservation_id}`. It never consumes step-up for a missing, stale or mismatched reservation. A consumed step-up is never restored on send failure.
3. Immediately before dispatch the tablet atomically verifies the reservation is unused and strictly before its local expiry, and rechecks attachment, lease, grant and policy; it marks the reservation used before invoking wipe. Expiry, release, disconnect, restart or invalidation destroys unused reservations. Duplicate reserve returns the same reservation only while live; duplicate execute never dispatches again. A failed or lost response or send cannot cause automatic replay; an uncertain dispatched wipe is `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`.

The relay deadline bounds the relay's execute write; the reservation's independently established tablet deadline bounds tablet dispatch even if execute is delayed. No synchronized clocks or transit bound are assumed for wipe, and neither deadline claims to be the same instant on both machines. The transit assumption remains applicable to ordinary commands only.

## Acceptance tests:

- Advance tablet and relay monotonic clocks independently: grant expires after 300 seconds despite substantive operations and lease renewals; a new attachment and Allow are required. Wall-clock jumps have no effect.
- Expire a status lease, a kiosk lease and a kiosk grant, and use local Stop. Verify immediate local cleanup and the specified event; deliver duplicates and delayed events after replacement and verify the replacement survives. Drop the connection before delivery and verify disconnect cleanup.
- Use different envelope IDs for a duplicate command and the same envelope ID for distinct command IDs: correlation and dedup remain separate. Deliver a matching terminal ack after release and after `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`; verify audit reconciliation without reviving the attachment or changing the reported result. Reject wrong device, socket, attachment or command IDs.
- Delay `reserved` past the relay deadline; revoke or change scope before execute; fail audit or bounded send: no unauthorized execute is sent, and consumed step-up is not restored. Delay execute beyond the tablet reservation deadline, replay execute, and invalidate or disconnect between phases: no wipe. A valid reservation and execute dispatch wipe once. Delay ordinary commands within and beyond their existing TTL/transit conditions.

## Interfaces touched:

- **I02 writer commit:** reserve is not step-up consumption; execute uses its final authorization → consume → durable audit → bounded send ordering, with no second generic check of the consumed step-up.
- **I04 frame fence:** terminal events and local expiry invalidate pending captures, frames and display references through publication.
- **I05 enrollment:** enrollment opening invalidates attachment and unused reservations; credential activation and cleanup remain independent.
- **I06 publication:** no publication policy change; protocol schema, fixtures and implementation ship only through its existing gates.
